# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

Be constructive, assume good faith, respect privacy, and focus criticism on ideas and code. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, publishing private information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
